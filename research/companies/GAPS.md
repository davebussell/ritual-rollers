# Companies: what's missing (2026-10-27)

120 companies built from two research passes (makers.json: 72 makers; builders-indoor-schools.json: 49 builders,
indoor parks and schools, one dropped for having no link of its own). Each has its own link, a source and dated
2025–26 activity.

- **Mexico** (5 in all): Mexican skate brands (Tricolor, Catrina, Raíz, Copal, Catarsis Wheels, Sindicato, Shivers
  Trucks, Opium Street Supply, ARRE, Deza) seem to live on Instagram and Mercado Libre with no readable site or base
  city; Aerobmx (BMX parts) lists only Facebook. No Mexican skatepark or pump track builder with a readable site and
  recent activity was found. Lead: Fishbone Sk8 (fishbone.com.mx) builds ramps.
- **Canada**: no BMX or protective-gear makers; roller is Jackson Ultima only, scooters North Scooters only.
- **Builders**: no BMX-track builder with a verifiable site. Rydan Ramp Company (Canadian Cycling Magazine, Dec 2025)
  has no site of its own, so it's left out.
- **Indoor parks**: thin in Atlantic Canada, the Prairies outside Calgary, and the US South and Midwest.
- **Schools**: no inline-first school; roller from three only.
- Left out for no published base city: Santa Cruz Skateboards / Independent (NHS), Banshee, Norco, Deity, Root
  Industries, Animal, Quasi, StrangeLove, Frog, Hopps, Ace, Krux, Mutiny, Merritt, Rollerbones, Phoenix Pro Scooters.
