# Photo permission requests

Ritual Rollers, 27 October 2026. 103 photos and videos of people riding 40 featured parks (14 in Canada, 18 in the US, 8 in Mexico): 45 Flickr photos and 58 YouTube videos. None of them can be used without a yes. The same list is in `permission-requests.json`.

## Before you send

- **Open the link and look first.** I picked these from titles, captions and channel names; I did not view the photos or videos. Skip any where the rider looks like a child, nobody is riding, or the park is not the one named.
- **Flickr** photos are "all rights reserved" (one is CC BY-NC, which also needs a yes). FlickrMail needs you signed in to a Flickr account.
- **YouTube** has no private messages. Where the channel links an Instagram, DM that account; otherwise leave a comment on the video. For a video, ask for one still frame.
- Keep each "yes" (a screenshot is fine) and use the credit they ask for.
- Paste the English message for most; there are French and Spanish versions for Montreal and Mexico.

## The message

Fill in `{name}`, `{park}` and `{link}`. Keep the line in brackets only for videos. For FlickrMail, use the subject line.

**Subject:** Your {park} photo: OK to feature it on Ritual Rollers?

> Hi {name},
>
> I'm Dave, and I run ritualrollers.com, a free guide to skateparks, pump tracks, BMX tracks and bike parks across Canada, the US and Mexico. I came across your shot from {park} ({link}) and it's exactly the kind of real riding we want people to see.
>
> Would you be OK with us showing it on the {park} page at ritualrollers.com? [It's a video, so we'd use one still frame from it.] It would be credited with your name and a link back to you. It's free, and we'll take it down any time you ask.
>
> If that works, just reply "yes" and tell me how you'd like to be credited.
>
> Thanks either way, and keep riding!
> Dave Bussell, ritualrollers.com
